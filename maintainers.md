# Micronaut Maintainers

A Maintainer is considered a member of the [Micronaut Core Developer Team](https://github.com/orgs/micronaut-projects/teams/core-developers).

## Eligibility

A contributor may become a Maintainer if they:

- Have demonstrated sustained and valuable contributions.
- Have actively participated in the community for at least six months.
- Have shown good technical judgment and collaboration skills.
- Agree to follow the project's [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Appointment

A candidate may be nominated by any active Maintainer.

Appointment requires approval from at least two-thirds of active Maintainers.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review pull requests regularly.
- Participate in governance discussions.
- Help onboard new contributors.
- Protect the project's long-term interests.

## Inactive Maintainers

A Maintainer who has not participated in project activities for six consecutive months may be designated as Inactive.

Inactive Maintainers:

- Retain public recognition.
- Do not participate in votes.
- May return to active status upon request and approval.


## Project Access

Micronaut uses [GitHub Teams](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/organizing-members-into-teams/about-teams) to grant commit access to repositories. The whole framework is maintained by the [Core Developer Team](https://github.com/orgs/micronaut-projects/teams/core-developers), whose members have the maintain role on repositories.

**Administrators**: A subset of the Core Developer Team belongs to a secret GitHub team with the admin role on every repository.

Some repositories have top contributors with write access. Moreover, some of these repositories (e.g., [micronaut-openapi](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-openapi), [micronaut-acme](https://github.com/micronaut-projects/micronaut-acme)) are maintained by those contributors.
